Q. What service will you build?
A. It will be a Diary App/Web Service.
   Users can write their diaries every day through this service.

Q. How will you build this?
A. I will use Django, Python, and Flutter.
   And I will develop this app alone.

Q. What functions will it have?
A. I envision four main features:

1. Writing and reading the diaries you’ve written.

2. ChatGPT will recommend diary topics, summarize your entries, 
   and analyze your emotions based on the content.

3. The service will provide insights, 
   such as how your emotions have changed recently and how often you write in your diary, 
   based on your personal data.

4. If possible, I will add a communication feature to connect with other people.
